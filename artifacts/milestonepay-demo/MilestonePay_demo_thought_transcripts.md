# MilestonePay — Demo user-thought transcripts

These are scripted user-perspective thoughts for a staged walkthrough. The recordings visibly identify their Fuji agreement, wallet, evidence, and settlement states as simulated for a deterministic demo.

## Video 1 — Client

00:00 → I want to hire someone, but I don't want to send the money directly and just hope the work gets delivered.

00:04 → I'll define the agreement first. The deliverable and the people involved should be explicit before funds move.

00:11 → I can see exactly which provider wallet will be paid, and an arbiter is already defined in case we disagree.

00:15 → The wallet confirmation is important — the application cannot move my funds without my approval.

00:20 → The money is committed, but it hasn't been released to the provider yet. The provider can verify the funds before doing any work.

## Video 2 — Provider

00:00 → Before I start working, I want proof the client actually committed the money.

00:05 → The agreement is addressed to my wallet, the amount is fixed, and payment is tied to the milestone.

00:09 → I've finished the work, so I submit the milestone instead of asking the client to manually send me money.

00:15 → Submitting does not automatically pay me. The client still has to review the milestone.

00:23 → After the client approves, the escrow releases the payment to my wallet. Payment follows the agreement state instead of relying on a promise.

## Video 3 — Arbiter

00:00 → I shouldn't be involved in normal payments. I only enter the flow when the client and provider disagree.

00:06 → I can compare committed evidence from both sides instead of relying on an editable chat message.

00:13 → Based on the evidence in this demo, I decide the milestone was delivered and release the escrow to the provider.

00:18 → The dispute now has a clear final state recorded by the protocol instead of remaining an off-platform argument.
